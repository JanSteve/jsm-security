## 💸 100% Free Technology Stack (Zero-Cost Setup)

- **AI Reasoning:** Google Gemini 2.0 Flash (`gemini-2.0-flash` free tier via Google AI Studio).
- **Failover AI:** OpenRouter Free Open Models (`openrouter/free`).

- **Live Database & CRM:** Google Sheets (Multi-tab JSM AI Command Center).
