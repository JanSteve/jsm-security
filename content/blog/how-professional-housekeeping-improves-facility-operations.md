When housekeeping is treated as random broom-and-mop labor, facility managers spend their entire week fielding complaints about dirty restrooms, slippery granite floors, and empty soap dispensers.

When housekeeping is managed as a **system**, clean workspaces happen automatically through scheduled operational cycles:

---

## 1. Hourly Restroom Inspection Protocol

Documented hourly sign-off sheets posted on doors, verified by the shift lead. Every check includes mirror polishing, sanitizing tap handles, restocking tissue and soap, and mopping floor splashes with disinfectant.

## 2. Color-Coded Microfiber Cloths

- **Red**: Restroom urinals, toilet bowls, and sanitary tiles.
- **Yellow**: Cafeteria tables, kitchen prep areas, and water cooler stations.
- **Blue**: Executive desks, glass partitions, and conference room electronics.

This eliminates bacteria transfer from washrooms to corporate workstations.

## 3. Periodic Deep Machine Scrubbing

High-speed ride-on and walk-behind scrubbing machines scheduled during weekend downtime for tiled and granite lobbies. Heavy industrial scrubbers lift embedded grime that traditional manual mopping merely smears around.

## 4. Eco-Friendly Cleaning Consumables

Safe, non-corrosive chemical dilutions that protect expensive stone surfaces while ensuring medical-grade hygiene. Standardized dilution dispensers eliminate chemical wastage and hazardous fumes.
