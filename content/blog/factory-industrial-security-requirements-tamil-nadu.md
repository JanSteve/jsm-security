In manufacturing units and industrial plants across Tamil Nadu's industrial belts (Coimbatore, Chennai, Hosur, Trichy, and Sriperumbudur), material shrinkage at exit gates represents a major hidden cost. Raw copper, brass fittings, tools, and finished components can leak out unless gate discipline is airtight.

A professional industrial security framework includes:

---

## 1. Dual Gate Pass System

No raw material, finished product, or scrap leaves the premises without a signed, serialized gate pass matching the dispatch invoice. Security officers must physically verify truck seals, weighbridge slips, and material quantities before opening boom barriers.

## 2. Physical Inspection of Departing Labor

Courteous, respectful bag and body searches for shift workers leaving production floors prevent small components and tooling from leaving lockers. Screening areas must be well-lit and recorded on high-definition CCTV.

## 3. Visitor & Contractor Tagging

External contractor labor must wear colored visitor badges distinguishing them from permanent employees. Clear visitor boundaries prevent unauthorized personnel from wandering near CNC machines or chemical stores.

## 4. Night Perimeter Vigilance

Active vigilance along boundary walls adjacent to scrap yards and loading docks eliminates after-hours throwing of goods over fencing. Security marshals must conduct continuous exterior patrols with powerful tactical torches.
