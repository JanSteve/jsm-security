At JSM Integrated Services, we do not deploy untrained personnel. Every guard and facility team member completes our structured 5-day induction program before stepping onto a client's facility:

---

## Day 1 — Values, Grooming & Discipline

Uniform turnout, personal hygiene, shift punctuality, and client confidentiality. Recruits learn why uniform neatness and military salutes establish authority and respect.

## Day 2 — Post Duties & Visitor Logging

Gate control, delivery vehicle checks, material registers, and polite phone etiquette. Marshals practice logging vehicle license numbers, handling courier packages, and inspecting driver licenses.

## Day 3 — Fire Safety & First Aid

Operating fire extinguishers using the PASS technique (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep), identifying emergency exit paths, handling electrical fire emergencies, and providing basic casualty support.

## Day 4 — Site-Specific SOPs

Tailoring duty actions to hospitals (infection control and quiet zones), IT offices (access cards and laptop passes), residential societies, or heavy industrial units (PPE compliance and weighbridge registers).

## Day 5 — Supervised Field Shadowing

Spending an entire 8-hour shift under an experienced field officer before independent placement. The trainee observes real-time visitor influx, shift handovers, and emergency radio drills.
