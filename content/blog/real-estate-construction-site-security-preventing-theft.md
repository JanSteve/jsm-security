Construction sites are among the most vulnerable commercial assets due to open perimeters, constant contractor movement, and high-value materials (copper wiring, TMT steel bars, cement, and sanitary fittings) stored in temporary sheds.

Effective construction site security requires:

---

## 1. Material Inward / Outward Registers

Every truck carrying steel, sand, or cement is weighed and cross-verified against delivery challans. Gate security logs driver phone numbers, truck license plates, and dispatch timestamps.

## 2. Labor Gate Identity Tagging

All subcontractor laborers are tagged with daily wristbands or token IDs to prevent unauthorized entry. Worker bag checks upon evening dismissal stop small hand tools and brass valves from leaking out.

## 3. Night Patrol & Spotlight Checks

Roving guards inspecting perimeter fencing, tool storage rooms, and electrical switchgear sheds. Unannounced supervisory night rounds ensure guards remain awake through 3:00 AM vulnerability windows.
