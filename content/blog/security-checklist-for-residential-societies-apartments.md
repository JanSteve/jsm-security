Managing security in a residential apartment complex or gated community requires balancing strict perimeter access control with a polite, resident-friendly atmosphere.

Here is the standard operating checklist every Apartment Resident Welfare Association (RWA) should implement:

---

## 1. Main Gate & Vehicle Ingress

- **Lane Segregation**: Maintain strict separation between resident FASTag/RFID lanes and visitor entry lanes to prevent vehicular congestion.
- **Delivery Traffic Protocol**: Mandatory logging of all delivery executive details (Name, delivery app, destination flat number).
- **Domestic Staff Authentication**: Domestic helpers, drivers, and daily cleaners must have photo ID passes registered with the society office.

---

## 2. Night Perimeter & Lighting Audits

- **Zero Blind Spots**: Ensure all perimeter boundary walls have working night illumination and zero blind corners.
- **Scheduled Patrols**: Physical guard patrols every 90 minutes between 11:00 PM and 05:00 AM with checkpoint logging.
- **DG Set & Pump Room Vigilance**: Securing generator rooms, overhead tank access ladders, and electrical panels from unauthorized tampering.

---

## 3. Material & Contractor Movement

- **Designated Working Hours**: Enforce designated working hours for interior carpenter/mason contractors to protect quiet hours.
- **Move-In / Move-Out Passes**: Gate passes for all major household shifting and appliance removals signed by flat owners or RWA executives.
